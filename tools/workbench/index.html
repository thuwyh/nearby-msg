Quick guide

Alex needs help with a leaking kitchen sink. Jordan and Sam are plumbers on Nearby; Nearby Support is the platform's customer service team.

  1. As Customer, describe the leak to Nearby Support below — type, hold 🎤 to talk, or add a photo or video.
  2. Switch to Platform to read it and reply. Switch back to Customer to see the reply.
  3. The Professional view switches between Jordan and Sam. They have no conversations yet.

All views share one local server. Messages survive refresh and restart. No real accounts or external messages.

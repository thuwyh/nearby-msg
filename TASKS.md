# Nearby Chat

**30 minutes of coding · 3 scenarios · choose 1 · AI welcome**

Alex needs a plumber. Help him communicate with professionals and Nearby Support.

## Your starting point

A working text conversation between Alex and Nearby Support, with Customer, Professional and Platform views. Jordan and Sam start without conversations. Use the existing support conversation where it fits your scenario.

## Choose one scenario

Explain the user problem you see and what you will prioritize. Ask questions, choose the interactions and scope, and deliver a working version. You do not need to solve every possible case.

### S1 · Help Alex explain the problem

Alex has a leaking kitchen sink. He wants the other person to understand what is happening without a long back-and-forth. Improve the chat experience to help him describe the problem more clearly.

### S2 · Talk to several professionals

Alex wants to contact Jordan and Sam to compare their advice and availability. He will move back and forth between conversations. Make that experience clear and easy to use.

### S3 · Help when messaging goes wrong

Alex sometimes has a poor connection or notices he has sent the wrong information. He needs to understand what happened to his message and what he can do next. Improve that experience.

## How to work

Start with a brief explanation of your approach. Use any AI tools you like. Finish by demonstrating a complete user flow, your verification, and the tradeoffs you made.

## What to deliver

- Make your chosen flow work through the UI and backend; keep existing text messaging working.
- Keep people’s conversations and data separate, and preserve saved messages across refresh and restart.
- Write your own checks. Explain what works, what you left out, and why.
